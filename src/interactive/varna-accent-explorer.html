About the Varna 93M Research & Word Engine

The Core Dilemma: Speech phone recognition models are scored against citation G2P dictionaries (like espeak-ng). These labels assert rigid, formal citation forms.

In natural speech across 12 Indic languages, real speakers produce structured phonological variation:

  • Eastern Sibilants: Bengali (/ʃ/ as in shomoy), Assamese (/x/ as in xokolo), Odia (/s/ as in samasta).
  • Dravidian Deaspiration: Kannada and Telugu casually drop aspiration in words like ghana ➔ gana, phala ➔ pala.
  • Tamil ழ (zha): /ɻ/ shifts to /ɭ/ (Tamizh ➔ Tamil) or /j/ (vazhi ➔ vayi).
  • Nativization: /z/ ➔ /dʒ/ (Zindagi ➔ Jindagi) and /f/ ➔ /pʰ/ (Saaf ➔ Saaph).
  • Retroflex Nasals: /ɳ/ weakens to [n] in words like baanam and paani.

Full-Word Speech Engine: This interactive lab synthesizes complete multi-syllable spoken utterances with precise acoustic formant contours, allowing you to hear both forms clearly in lexical context.
