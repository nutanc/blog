Shape alignment is the cosine similarity of two films' standardized 20-position × 13-axis arcs (0.5+ is close in this space; the ranking is what matters). "Because" lists the rarest traits the two films share and the lines that move together. Act markers are the conventional quarter points; the climax marker is this film's tension peak, the reveal marker is where its open puzzle collapses. The master lines are lightly smoothed for reading (each underlying point is a 1–4 rating); the facet sparklines are raw. Genre tags from IMSDb where available; Indian scripts tagged by language.
